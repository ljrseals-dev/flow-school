    About

    Spelling-based heuristics can be wrong on names, slang and unusual words. Lessons draw on general vocal-health guidance (for example Johns Hopkins Medicine's vocal hygiene sheet for singers and Furtado's School of Music tips for young singers) and common music-teaching practice. Reference artists are style inspirations only: nothing here clones or imitates anyone's voice or reproduces lyrics.
